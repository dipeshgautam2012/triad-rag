Triad RAG is a small retrieval stack with chunkers and indexers.

Chunkers split documents into passages. The simple chunker uses fixed-size windows with overlap.

Indexers store those passages and answer search queries. ChromaIndexer embeds text and runs similarity search. Bm25Indexer indexes keywords for lexical search.

Upload a file, chunk it, index it, then query the index to get the most relevant passages.
